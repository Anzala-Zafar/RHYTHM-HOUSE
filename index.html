Our Story

Rhythm House was founded in 1995 with a simple mission: to bring music to everyone. What started as a small shop with just a few hundred albums has grown into the city's premier music destination.

We pride ourselves on having the largest collection of regional and devotional music, along with an extensive selection of international artists. Whether you're looking for the latest pop album or a hard-to-find 60s rock classic, we're here to help.

What We Offer

  • CDs, DVDs, and tapes across all genres
  • Music books and magazines (including international editions)
  • Special orders for hard-to-find albums
  • Live shows and music events
  • Contests and special promotions
  • Membership program with exclusive benefits
